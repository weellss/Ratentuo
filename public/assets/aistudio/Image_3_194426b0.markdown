## Brand & Style

This design system powers a high-stakes, real-time reverse auction and lowest-unique-bid platform. The experience blends calculated game theory with the electrical thrill of live gaming.

### Persona & Atmosphere
- **Calculated & Game-Theoretic:** Interfaces feel structured, analytical, and fair. Data-dense feeds and real-time ledger records project architectural legitimacy.
- **Electric Anticipation:** High-contrast focal cues signal shifting dynamics, unique state changes, and imminent countdown terminations without visual chaos.
- **Refined Tactility:** Physical metaphors surface through responsive lift elevations, distinct structural borders, and punchy, tactile interactive surfaces.

### Visual Architecture
Drawing from Modern Neobrutalism tempered with luxury game-table polish, the system rejects washed-out generic SaaS tones in favor of a warm parchment canvas (`Ivory`), hyper-focused dark zones (`Deep Ink`), royal game accents (`Royal Purple`), and high-energy spotlight callouts (`Sunshine`).

## Colors

The palette leverages high chromatic contrast to communicate hierarchy, auction states, and instant actionable intelligence.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#FFD23F` - Sunshine):** High-alert active state indicator. Reserved exclusively for primary call-to-action buttons (with `#1B1330` text), current lowest unique bid badges, winning outcome highlights, and critical real-time alerts.
- **Secondary (`#4B2E83` - Royal Purple):** Structural anchor. Powers bidding board containers, headers, ledger columns, and secondary actions (with pure white `#FFFFFF` text).
- **Tertiary (`#8B84A3` - Lavender Grey):** Calibrated intermediate tone. Directs secondary metadata, bid timestamps, inactive states, and subtle layout divisions.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1B1330` - Deep Ink):** Core textual content, high-contrast dark sections, and structural focal points.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FBF8F1` - Ivory):** The warm, non-sterile foundation for base screens.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Reserved for elevated bidding cards, metric tiles, and input surfaces.

### Border Tints & Transparency
- Surface borders utilize `rgba(75, 46, 131, 0.12)` for standard card edges and `rgba(75, 46, 131, 0.35)` for focused or hovered cards.
- Bidding boards and active live modules feature structural `rgba(75, 46, 131, 1.0)` bounding contours.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is divided into three distinct operational roles:

1. **Space Grotesk (Headings & Structural Titles):** Geometric, technological, and confident. Handles structural auction anchors, card titles, section headers, and promotional banners.
2. **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & Microcopy):** Clear, approachable, and highly readable across varying scales. Handles user guidance, legal terms, card descriptions, and operational instructions.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Quantitative & Temporal Streams):** Strictly reserved for numerical telemetry: countdown timers, currency amounts ($0.01 increments), bid serial IDs, ledger table rows, and uniqueness indicators. Monospace ensures zero horizontal layout shifts as numbers rapidly tick down.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model runs on a modular 8pt mathematical rhythm using a 12-column responsive fluid grid.

### Breakpoints & Grids
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Bidding boards and analytical live charts occupy asymmetric 8:4 splits.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Side modules reflow underneath active auction boards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1rem`, `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem`. Auction stats stack vertically into single-column cards with persistent sticky bid action bars docked to the bottom viewport.

### Component Spacing Rhythm
- **Internal Card Padding:** `space-lg` (24px) for desktop live auction cards; `space-md` (16px) for mobile.
- **Metric Row Insets:** `space-sm` (8px) between tabular bid amounts, status badges, and user counts.
- **Board Grid Gap:** `space-md` between discrete bid tiles within the heat-map matrix.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines physical layer lifting with tinted purple rim-light contours.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Floor - `#FBF8F1`):** Structural backdrops, table canvas, and global wrapper containers.
- **Level 1 (Default Card - `#FFFFFF`):** Base auction cards, statistical panels, and inactive ledger entries. Bounded by a crisp 1px stroke: `rgba(75, 46, 131, 0.12)`. Subtle resting shadow: `0 2px 4px rgba(27, 19, 48, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Elevated Card):** When an auction card is hovered or focused, it translates upward by `-4px` (`transform: translateY(-4px)`). Shadow expands dynamically to `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(75, 46, 131, 0.14), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(27, 19, 48, 0.06)`, and border tint intensifies to `rgba(75, 46, 131, 0.35)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Live Overlay / Bottom Bid Dock):** High-priority panels feature crisp containment shadows: `0 20px 38px -10px rgba(27, 19, 48, 0.28), 0 0 0 1px rgba(75, 46, 131, 0.2)`.

### Dark Sections & Bidding Boards
Dark interactive regions (`#1B1330`) rely on tonal surface layering (`#251A40` for inner tiles) paired with internal inset highlights (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`) rather than ambient dropshadows.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a precise 12px (`0.75rem`) border-radius for cards, input containers, and structural auction blocks, matching `roundedness: 2` with calculated micro-adjustments:

- **Cards & Bidding Modules:** 12px (`0.75rem`).
- **Buttons & Numerical Input Fields:** 8px (`0.5rem`) for compact control, up to 12px for hero action triggers.
- **Pills, Countdown Bubbles & Unique Status Badges:** Fully rounded capsule curves (`9999px`) to immediately separate operational tags from rigid layout blocks.
- **Borders:** Consistent 1px crisp strokes across all cards; 1.5px solid strokes on inputs and active bid boards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Place Bid", "Confirm Bid"):** 
  - Background: `#FFD23F` (Sunshine).
  - Text: `#1B1330` (Deep Ink), `Space Grotesk` 600 weight.
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(27, 19, 48, 0.2)`.
  - Hover: Background `#FFE066`, translation `-2px`, shadow `0 6px 16px rgba(255, 210, 63, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary Action ("View Ledger", "Auto-Bid Options"):**
  - Background: `#4B2E83` (Royal Purple).
  - Text: `#FFFFFF`, `Space Grotesk` 600 weight.
  - Hover: Background `#5C39A1`, shadow `0 4px 12px rgba(75, 46, 131, 0.3)`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Action:**
  - Background: Transparent.
  - Text: `#4B2E83`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(75, 46, 131, 0.25)`.
  - Hover: Background `rgba(75, 46, 131, 0.05)`, border `#4B2E83`.

### Auction Cards
- **Base State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 12px radius, 1px border `rgba(75, 46, 131, 0.12)`, padding `space-lg`.
- **Hover Interaction:** Smooth transition (`200ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), `-4px` vertical lift, intensified purple border `rgba(75, 46, 131, 0.35)`.
- **Header:** Title in `#1B1330` (`Space Grotesk`), auction timer in `JetBrains Mono` nestled inside a pill container with a pulsing amber beacon.

### Lowest Unique Bid Highlighting & Chips
- **Unique & Lowest (Winning State):** Filled `#FFD23F` chip with `#1B1330` text in `JetBrains Mono` bold. Framed by a subtle animated amber halo.
- **Unique But Not Lowest:** `#F3EFFC` background, `#4B2E83` text, `rgba(75, 46, 131, 0.2)` border.
- **Duplicate / Non-Unique Bid:** `#F1F0F5` background, `#8B84A3` text, cross-out glyph indicator.

### Input Fields (Bid Entry)
- Currency input containers feature a fixed currency prefix (`$`) in `#8B84A3` and bid values rendered strictly in `JetBrains Mono` 600.
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` with 1.5px border `rgba(75, 46, 131, 0.2)`.
- Focus State: Border color `#4B2E83`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(75, 46, 131, 0.15)`, outline none.

### The Bidding Matrix / Board
- An interactive numerical heat-grid mapped inside a `#1B1330` dark-surface enclosure.
- Individual bid cells: 8px radius, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Color states mapped precisely to auction rules:
  - Gold (`#FFD23F`) = Current Lowest Unique Bid.
  - Soft Purple (`#6E4BAE`) = User's Placed Bids.
  - Lavender Grey Strikethrough (`#8B84A3`) = Contested / Duplicate bids.
